OPERATION
PACIFIC SHIELD

Take control of an OPS station. Computer-controlled teammates fill vacant positions. Coordinate RFIs, CAOC allocation, loading, the ATO, space support, medical movement, and turn execution.

Fictional practice environment. Not an official OPS adjudication tool.

OPS PLAY GUIDE

1. Review the mission order and political access before planning.

2. MOB selects the initial five RFIs. The simulated GM responds during execution.

3. MOB requests an aircraft; CAOC allocates it.

4. MOB loads counters, prepares the ATO with destination, alternate, and intent, and submits it.

5. CAOC reviews the package and issues simulated clearance/PPR.

6. Execute the turn to animate approved aircraft and resolve supported actions.

7. Review the log and debrief. AI operates all unoccupied stations.

Only the rules positively verified from the supplied guide are represented as guide-derived. Unverified task adjudications remain unavailable.
